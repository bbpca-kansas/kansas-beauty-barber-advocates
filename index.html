01 Why we’re speaking up

Consumers deserve to know. Professionals deserve a voice.

The Kansas Beauty and Barber Professionals Consumer Advocates is a consumer-focused advocacy group. Our petition calls for full disclosure to consumers before unregulated beauty services are provided, and for the people in the industry to be heard in decisions that affect their work.
